## Goal
A deleted, forged or replayed entry can no longer lift a pause. Pause state becomes one sealed observed-remove document of pause elements and tombstones whose content only ever grows by union, written by compare-and-set and merged on every read and every split-brain heal. A member counts as fresh only while one gate, judged on what it holds, its peers' card digests and its own clock, has passed within the last lease. A document that is absent, unauthentic or not fresh means "everything paused" (design 2.3.3, OD-3, OD-4, OD-14, OD-15). Deleting the document becomes a visible denial of service, which is the safe direction.

## Invariants
- **F0.** Content only ever unions. No accept, merge, restore or repair removes an element or a tombstone. The only way to lift a pause is a tombstone written by a resume or an `endScope`.
- **F1.** A member M is fresh at time t only if, at some renewal time within the last lease L before t, M passed the single gate:
  - the stored document is authentic and M lacks nothing from it (M has merged all of it), **and**
  - **Covered**: every tracked peer shows a valid card at the latest completed cards look (F5), and that card's digest is in Seen(M), meaning M holds that content now, has held it, or has merged it as a stored document. A valid no-document card carries no digest and passes with nothing to check (R7). A tracked peer with no valid card at that look fails Covered *(197-20: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 3, owner may override)*.
  
  The bound on how long a member that stops being covered can stay fresh is W = L + Tp + A. Tp is the maximum time a peer's valid card can show content other than what that peer holds. While card writes succeed and the schedule thread is not held up, Tp ≤ L/2. A look queued ahead of a card pass on the same single-thread executor delays the card by that look's duration, which 227 does not bound, so this figure holds only while looks finish promptly; whatever the delay, the old card counts only while it is valid, so Tp ≤ L + `clockSkewTolerance` below holds in every case (197-86: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after external review of PR #197 round 10, owner may override). In the prompt case: the card is published on change (R7), and the half-lease refresh bounds the delay when a change-triggered write is lost or coalesced. When a peer's card writes are deleted or blocked, its last card counts only while it is inside its lease (F5), so Tp ≤ L + `clockSkewTolerance`. A is the maximum accepted age of the cards look the gate judged on, at most one lease (R6). So W = L + Tp + A ≤ 3 L + `clockSkewTolerance` *(197-8: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 1, owner may override; 197-19: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 2, owner may override; 197-20: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 3, owner may override)*.

The sequence, the refresh counter, the issuer, and who wrote the bytes are never evidence. There is no `renew` flag. Every lease reset goes through the gate, at one of the renewal points listed in R6.
- **F3.** Unverified members never gate freshness for an established member, except through peer state unknown: a member that joins or leaves makes peer state unknown until a look completes (R6), so membership churn by any member, an unverified one included, can delay an established member's renewal (197-78: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after external review of PR #197 round 9, owner may override). `unverified-member.action: alert` and the unverified grace period affect only `MemberCards`' serving status. `pause.fresh` is independent of them. ## Rules

### R1. The document
- One document under a fixed keyed name in `dataprism.oversight.s1`, sealed under the cluster key. Its plaintext holds `sequence`, `refresh`, `issuedAt`, the issuer, a format tag, and two sets:
  - `elements`: each has an `id` of 128 random bits from a `SecureRandom`, a target kind (`ALL`, `TOOL` or `SCOPE`) and a name (empty for `ALL`);
  - `tombstones`: ids.
- A target is paused iff some element for it has no tombstone. These are its "live ids". The `OversightSnapshot` is derived from them, and its shape does not change.
- Decode rejects the document as UNREADABLE, counted as tamper, for:
  - an unknown format tag;
  - a duplicate id with a different target;
  - a list count above `1 << 20` (`MAX_ENTRIES`, the outer guard);
  - more than 65,536 elements, or more than 65,536 tombstones (the format bound, R5).
- Any authentic document at or below the format bound opens, whatever the operational cap. A tombstone whose id names no element in the same document is kept, not dropped: it blocks a later replay of that element. So decode does not reject a document with more tombstones than elements, or with tombstones for ids it does not hold; it bounds each set separately.
- `sequence` and `refresh` order writes (the commit guard, R4) and `sequence` feeds the card *(197-27: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 3, owner may override)*. They are never freshness evidence (F2).

### R2. Merge on acceptance
- Accepting any authentic document sets `held = held ⊔ doc`: the union of the element sets and the union of the tombstone sets. Acceptance never replaces held content, whatever the order.
- A document whose id names a different target from the same id in held is not accepted. Held is unchanged, the document is counted as tamper, and one rate-limited WARN is logged with no content.
- Held's metadata after a merge *(197-4: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 1, owner may override)*:
  - its order (`sequence`, `refresh`) is the lexicographic maximum of the orders of the merged documents, held included;
  - its `issuedAt` and issuer are those of the max-order document. On an order tie between documents with different `issuedAt` or issuer, the later `issuedAt` wins, then the issuer greater as encoded bytes, so merge stays commutative *(the tie-break is a coordinator detail)*;
  - so held's order never decreases, and the writes (R3, R4, R9, R11) and R13's `top` read these values.
- Merge is commutative, associative and idempotent, over the sets and the metadata alike. No merge removes a live element, except by a tombstone for its id.
- Every change of held appends `Digest(held)` to Seen (R6) and, for the main `HazelcastOversightState` document only (R7, R12), notifies `MemberCards`, which publishes a card when the reported digest or sequence changes (R7). The notice never writes: it only sets the pending mark in `Declarations` and wakes the per-instance schedule (R7, 197-74: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after external review of PR #197 round 9, owner may override) (IR-21: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after internal review of contract version 2.9, owner may override) *(197-45: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 5, owner may override)*. Merging an authentic stored document also appends that document's digest to Seen, whether or not held changed *(197-21: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 3, owner may override)*.
- Acceptance alone never resets the lease. Only a passing gate does (R6).

### R3. Every write is lock, read, compute from base
Under the oversight lock: re-read and judge the stored value, form base, and compute:
- **pause:** add one fresh element for the target. Always, even if the target is already live; pauses are never deduped.
- **resume:** the resume fixes its observed set `O` when it decides: the ids live for the target in the first base it reads under the lock. It tombstones `O ∩ live(base)`. If that set is empty, it writes nothing and returns success.
- **endScope:** the same observed-set rule, where `O` is the live ids of elements of kind `SCOPE` with that name. Elements of other kinds with the same name are untouched.
- `sequence = max(held.sequence, stored.sequence) + 1`, `refresh = 0`.
- No peer-derived term appears in any sequence: not in pause, resume, endScope, `pushHeld`, restore or `reconcileAfterMerge`. The `top = Math.max(top, peers.highestReportedSequence())` line and the `peers.highestReportedSequence() + 1` term of the earlier implementation do not exist (197-53: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after external review of PR #197 round 6, owner may override).
- A pause over an ABSENT document is `putIfAbsent(held ⊔ change)` at `held.sequence + 1`, or Doc(1){change} when `held == null`. This reconciles OD-215-16. Do not write `all = true` into the document.
- A lost race (the `replace` or `putIfAbsent` fails) re-reads and recomputes from the new base, up to `MAX_ATTEMPTS`, then fails closed. A resume or endScope applies every R13 precondition, in R13's order and with its codes, to every read, retries included: a retry that finds `held == null`, not serving, a member unverified, peer state unknown while not alone, a stored document that is ABSENT, UNREADABLE or GATED, or a failing gate at that read's `judge` is refused with that precondition's code *(197-47: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after external review of PR #197 round 6, owner may override)*. Whatever refuses it, it writes nothing, so it never trusts a member list that changed during the race, never falls back to `putIfAbsent` and never replaces a value R9 protects *(IR-2: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after internal review of contract version 2.4, owner may override; IR-12: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after internal re-review of contract version 2.5, owner may override)*. It keeps the `O` it fixed on its first read: on every retry it tombstones exactly `O ∩ live(new base)` and never adds an id first seen on a retry, so a pause that lands concurrently stays active. If the intersection is empty, it writes nothing and returns success.
- After its own commit, `acceptWritten` merges the written document into held and calls the gate as a post-write renewal point (R6). Neither `commit` nor `acceptWritten` takes a renew argument.
- Writes happen only in these places, never on the read path: the tick (repair, restore, refresh and first start; `pushHeld` is the tick's repair and restore write of R9 and runs only on the tick), an operator or `endScope` call, and `reconcileAfterMerge` on a `MERGED` event (R11). The gate itself writes nothing and may run on reads *(197-41: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after external review of PR #197 round 5, owner may override)*.

### R4. The commit guard
- `commit` refuses, and does not write, any `next` that fails one of these:
  - `next.elements ⊇ stored.elements` and `next.tombstones ⊇ stored.tombstones`, against the stored authentic document;
  - `next.elements ⊆ base.elements ∪ {the one fresh element of a pause}`;
  - `next.tombstones ⊆ base.tombstones ∪ T`. For a resume or endScope, `T` is `O ∩ live(base)`. For every other write `T` is empty: only a resume or an endScope adds a tombstone;
  - the order (sequence, then refresh) is above the stored document's, or `next` is a `refresh + 1` successor of it with identical sets.
- Each refusal is an `Outcome` the tests can see.

### R5. Operational cap and format bound
- The operational cap is judged on the merged base, `b = |base.elements|`, before the change is added, and applies only to a pause:
  - a non-`all` pause is refused with `CLUSTER_OPERATION_REFUSED` when `b + 1` would exceed 8,176;
  - `pause all` is accepted whenever `b + 1` stays within the format bound (65,536 elements), whether or not a merge happened. The reserve for `pause all` is the whole gap from 8,176 to 65,536, and only `pause all` may use it. The emergency pause is never blocked below the format bound.
- Tombstones are not counted. A resume or endScope is never refused by the cap: it only tombstones existing elements, and a cap on it would leave the cluster unable to lift a pause.
- The format bound, applied to encode and decode alike, is 65,536 elements and 65,536 tombstones. `1 << 20` stays as the outer per-list guard.
- A merge, restore, `pushHeld` or repair write of any authentic union within the format bound is always encodable and is written.
- An encode above the format bound is refused, never truncated. A decode above it is UNREADABLE and counted as tamper (R1).
- Size: a document built by local writes stays at or below 16,384 entries (8,176 non-`all` elements plus at most as many tombstones) except through `pause all` churn. Only that churn or a merge of split sides makes it larger, and both are bounded by the format bound.

### R8. First start
- A member with `held == null` that finds the document ABSENT may `putIfAbsent` an empty Doc(1) (no elements, no tombstones) only if all of these hold, judged under the oversight lock in the same decision as the write:

  - `unverifiedCount() == 0`: every member is verified;

Any offending look restarts the wait, which begins again at the first completed look after it; this is the one meaning of "restart" in R8 (197-39, IR-20, IR-26). First start reads this through `cleanRunAgeNanos()`, never through `peerDigests()` and never by keeping looks of its own *(IR-27: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after internal review of contract version 2.11, owner may override)*.
  - unless it is alone: `lookAgeNanos()` is at most L/2, so the latest look is no more than L/2 before the decision, and peer state is known (R6) at the decision, under the lock. Unknown peer state at the decision defers the write to a later decision; it does not restart the wait (IR-7, 197-31) *(IR-27: coordinator decision 2026-11-02 after internal review of contract version 2.11, owner may override)*.

### R10. Refresh writes
- A member that is fresh at the tick, whose stored document opens to exactly its held sets, writes a `refresh + 1` successor (identical sets, new `issuedAt`) once half a lease has passed, by its nano clock, since it first read the current stored bytes. This keeps `issuedAt` inside the cold-start gate (R6) and re-asserts the 0.7 fence (R16).
- A lapsed member never writes a refresh. Writing or reading a refresh renews nobody beyond what the gate gives (F2) *(planner reading of decision 5 of 2026-11-02; owner may override)*.

## Design-doc facts

The one current list of design-doc changes for the scribe, 213 and 261; this PR does not make them. Where it and the PR description differ, this list wins *(197-51: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after external review of PR #197 round 6, owner may override)*.
- `docs/plan/design/cluster-security.md` 1.9 (member cards): Card v2 adds `pauseDigest` (the first 16 bytes of SHA-256 over the count-prefixed sorted element ids, then the count-prefixed sorted tombstone ids) or a no-document marker, and a sealed per-instance `cardSequence`. `FORMAT_VERSION` is 2, and a version-1 card is rejected. The card is published on change and at half lease, with one write in flight per instance. A reader keeps, per listed member, the latest accepted `cardSequence` and the highest sighted one, with its first-sighting nano time and whether it was inside its lease then. A card is valid only by 227's check, the highest sighted sequence, a first sighting inside its lease, and `firstSeenNanos + L`.
- 2.3.3 (the pause document): an observed-remove set of elements and tombstones, merged by union on every read and heal. Writes are lock, read and compute from `held ⊔ stored`, under the commit guard. The cap is 8,176 non-`all` elements, and the format bound is 65,536 elements and 65,536 tombstones. Freshness is the single gate of F1 to F5: authentic stored content held, Covered over tracked peers, Seen including merged stored documents, the cold-start clause, and peer state unknown (no look, a stale look, or a member-set change). W ≤ 3 L + `clockSkewTolerance`. A restore is a union with no sequence condition, and refresh writes are not evidence. First start needs a member with `held == null` over an ABSENT document that is either alone in the member list (the lone-member exemption: no uptime wait and no look conditions) or the oldest member with one lease of uptime, every member verified at the decision, a clean run of looks spanning a full lease, the latest look at most L/2 old, and known peer state (IR-31: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after internal review of contract version 2.13, owner may override).
- 4.4: `pause.fresh` comes from the gate; `pause.age_seconds` is the time since the last passing gate; `pause.conflict` is redefined (R15).
- OD-8: `alert` and the unverified grace affect only `MemberCards` serving status, not `pause.fresh`. The cold-start clause uses the R7 unverified count, not 227's.
- OD-15 (i): a restore is a union, and the restorer's freshness comes from the gate.
- OD-14 already reflects owner decision (e) and needs no edit.
- Residuals: as listed in this brief's Residuals section.
- 261 (the operator API): resume returns `stillPaused`. Resume and endScope are refused with `CLUSTER_OPERATION_REFUSED` when not serving, when a member is unverified, or while peer state is unknown, and with `OVERSIGHT_UNAVAILABLE` for no held document, an ABSENT, UNREADABLE or GATED document, or a gate that fails at the call's own `judge`.
- 1.9 and the refusal codes: a wrapper whose pause lease, skew tolerance or cluster key ids differ from the instance's first wrapper is refused with `CLUSTER_SETTINGS_MISMATCH` (IR-33, owner decision 2026-11-03; IR-44: coordinator decision 2026-11-03 after internal review of contract version 2.16, owner may override).
