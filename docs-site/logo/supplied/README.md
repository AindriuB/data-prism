# Data Prism logo

Triangular prism direction: a front triangle and a visible side face, constructed directly from six paths. Flat projected geometry; no gradients, lighting or gloss. No generated bitmap tracing. Transparent mark and wordmark assets are supplied in both colourways.

- `mark-light.svg`: deep slate #1E293B for light surfaces.
- `mark-dark.svg`: light slate #E2E8F0 for dark surfaces.
- `wordmark-light.svg` / `wordmark-dark.svg`: mark with plain Arial / Helvetica / sans-serif lettering. Text remains editable and uses the installed font fallback; the mark has no font dependency.
- Matching PNGs: transparent raster exports for convenient use.
- `favicon-light-16.png` / `favicon-dark-16.png`: actual 16×16 exports; 32×32 versions also included.
- `preview.png`: both colourways, lockups and favicon proofs on contrasting backgrounds. View at 100% for actual favicon scale.

The three outgoing horizontal lines use #6366F1 at 100%, 85% and 70% opacity. No additional accent hues, gradients or filters are used. The prism and incoming ray are slate. The symbol is abstract rather than a literal optical ray diagram. At 16×16 the three-unit beam thickness becomes 0.75 pixels, and output centres are separated by two pixels. The 32px version retains the side face more clearly. The front and side contours are continuous; indigo lines begin beyond the sloping side face with a consistent gap. All three have equal length. Their angled ends and staggered positions follow the slope of the face, preserving a clear gap from the outline. Geometry is inset by a uniform 0.92 scale for padding.

The square has a 64×64 viewBox. Its geometry is identical at every export size. Do not add a shadow or glow. Use the dark variant on dark surfaces; transparent artwork does not itself select a colour scheme. This delivery does not change the live website.
